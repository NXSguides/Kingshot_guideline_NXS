65	🔬 Our research · updated {d}
66	🔎 What we saw
67	Troops that fall go one of three ways: lightly injured (out of this fight, but they stay with the march and can fight again — except when defending a building such as a Sanctuary, Outpost or King's Castle: there they are sent home — no healing needed — so the garrison gets thinner with each attack), injured (in the Infirmary, can be healed), or lost (gone).
68	In any battle, once your Infirmary is full, the injured that don't fit are lost. Apart from that, troops are lost only when you attack someone's Town (35% of the troops that fall) or fight over a Lv. 4 Outpost (10%).
69	When the defending side of a report shows losses, their Infirmary was full.
70	💡 Suggestions
71	Before you can be attacked, heal your injured so the Infirmary has room.
72	Holding a building: refill the garrison between attacks — the lightly injured have gone home.
73	Before attacking a Town, count on 35% of the fallen troops not coming back.
