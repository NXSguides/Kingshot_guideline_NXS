Max 70,080 troops per member.

ATTACK → DEFENSE
⚔️ 1. CAPTURE
Join the initial rally using your ATTACK preset.
Example: Amadeus / Chenko 50 : 20 : 30
🛡️ 2. SWITCH TO DEFENSE
Once the building is captured:
1. Immediately send another march using your DEFENSE preset. Example: Gordon / Howard 60 : 20 : 20
2. Watch your defense march travel toward the captured building.
3. When approximately 5 seconds of march time remain — or as instructed by leadership — recall your original ATTACK march.
4. Your DEFENSE march arrives and replaces your offensive march in the garrison.
🚫 DO NOT recall your attack march too early.
Capture → Send Defense → ~5 sec → Recall Attack → Defense Arrives
This allows us to transition from an offensive setup to a defensive garrison without unnecessarily weakening the building.

Only 15 governors can enter HQ / Sanctuary. So don’t automatically send your maximum march to HQ, Sanctuary, or other contested buildings.
Follow the troop cap announced by leadership.
Typical alliance target: ~70,080 troops per player

### GUIDE #hero-roulette — Hero Roulette
WHEN
Every 2 weeks, 3 days each time.
📋 RULES
The event lasts 3 days, with 1 free spin per day and up to 150 spins per day.
Each spin costs 1 Lucky Chip or 1,500 Gems; a 10-spin costs 13,500 Gems.
One-time milestone rewards at 5, 15, 35, 70 and 120 total spins give +5, +10, +20, +30 and +50 shards — 115 in total.
🎲 PRIZE ODDS
Mythic Hero Shard x5: 4.37%
Mythic Hero Shard x1: 32.81%
Mystery pack x1: 6.56% (two slots, 6.56% each)
1h general Speedup x1: 20.51%
10K Hero XP x1: 18.27%
Four resource slots (Iron 10K×20, Stone 10K×80, Wood 10K×400, Bread 10K×400): 2.73% each
Expected shards per spin: 5 × 0.0437 + 1 × 0.3281 ≈ 0.547
Expected shards after N spins (from spins + milestone rewards)
3 spins (free only): ~1.6
10 spins: ~5.5 + 5 = 10.5
15 spins: ~8.2 + 15 = 23
35 spins: ~19 + 35 = 54
70 spins: ~38 + 65 = 103
120 spins: ~66 + 115 = 181
After 120 spins there are no more milestone rewards and each spin only gives ~0.55 shards, so F2P and light spenders shouldn't go past 120.
🎯 How many spins to max one hero?
On average, about 720 spins — 6 events with 120 spins each.
The math
Maxing a hero (unlock + up to 5 stars) takes 1,075 shards in total.
120 spins per event gives about 0.5466 × 120 + 115 ≈ 181 shards on average (~0.5466 per spin plus 115 from the milestone rewards).
1,075 ÷ 181 ≈ 5.95, so you need 6 events.
Total: 6 × 120 = 720 spins, about 6 × 181 ≈ 1,084 shards expected.
Why not keep spinning in one event?
After 120 spins there are no more milestone rewards, so each spin only gives ~0.55 shards. Even at the 450-spin cap, one event only averages 0.5466 × 450 + 115 ≈ 361 shards — the extra 330 spins are only about half as efficient as the first 120.
Spin all 120 in the 6th event too
The first 5 events average ~903 shards, leaving ~172 to go. At 119 spins you'd only expect ~130 (65 + 65); you need the +50 pack at spin 120 to cover the rest, so the last event needs all 120 spins as well.
💎 Gems cost (not counting Lucky Chips)
After the 3 free spins, each event costs 159,000 Gems — about 954,000 Gems for all 6. Subtract any Lucky Chips you have or shards from other sources.
⚠️ These are averages. Over 720 spins the real result will usually land within ±60 shards of the expected value (about 95% of the time) — with bad luck you may need one more event, with good luck you may finish early.
⚔️ KvK TIP
In the KvK Preparation Phase, Days 2 and 3 give 8,000 points per Hero Roulette spin — so it's worth saving your Lucky Chips and spin Gems for those two days.

### GUIDE #tri-alliance-clash — Tri-Alliance Clash
🎲 HOW THE EVENT WORKS
Think of Tri-Alliance Clash as a board game with paths, buildings and battles. Your alliance moves through different lanes, captures territory and fights toward the highest-value objectives.
⚔️ BATTLE MECHANICS
Battles are auto-battles. Simulated battles are solo matches, NOT rallies.
Troop ratios cannot be changed — troops are split evenly.
March Speed and March Capacity bonuses DO NOT apply.
🛡️ 3 MARCHES & HERO SELECTION
You deploy 3 squads before entering the fight.
F2P players
Put your strongest heroes together.
Do NOT spread your strongest heroes across all 3 marches.
Build one strong march first.
P2W players
Use your offensive and defensive orange heroes.
Offensive Widgets DO NOT count in this event. Defensive Widgets DO count — prioritize heroes with strong defensive Widgets.
🏛️ BUILDINGS & POINTS
Temple of Tides = PRIMARY OBJECTIVE — it opens at the end.
Names used in our chat: Shrine = Cluster of Ruins, Altar = Ruins.
Attacking and capturing buildings gives more points than just defending/holding them — unlike Swordland Showdown.
👥 TEAMS & ROLES
Players are split into about 3 teams working different lanes — the strongest player plus a back-up for each target, so our strength is spread effectively.
Team 3 — Capture neutral buildings at the start
Capture the neutral buildings on our territory ASAP when the battle opens.
Once your target is secured, keep advancing.
Team 2 — Advance & opportunity captures
Keep moving ahead of weaker teams to the next target in your lane and keep the pressure on.
Watch enemy movement closely. When enemies leave a building to advance, move into the building they just left and capture it while it's undefended.
Use every opening like this to gain territory and points.
Team 1 — Push to the enemy Garrison
Main job: reach the enemy Garrison area before the 20-minute mark.
Keep advancing through your lane until you reach the enemy building closest to their Garrison (Shrine / Cluster of Ruins).
Be in position BEFORE the Garrison opens so we can capture it right at 20:00.
R4s
R4s and Legion commanders must actively manage Captain assignments throughout the battle.
Captains = the strongest players. They move across the map capturing targets, so they need the most Energy.
Keep checking whether strong players without the bonus should be made Captain.
Open the Captain screen by tapping the banners at the top left of the screen.
Energy generated for Captains per building:
Garrisons: 60,000 — gold
Cluster of Ruins (Shrine): 45,000 — purple
Ruins (Altar): 15,000 — blue

🔵 Blue = Transit Hub　🔴 Red = Garrisons　🟡 Yellow = Temple of Tides
Phase 1: Preparations (0:00–3:00)
Goal: set up your squads and get to know the map. Remember the purple, red and yellow zones — the ones you occupy turn blue. Check the enemies' strength and decide which alliance to focus on.

⚡ Energy MANAGEMENT

Instantly revive — recover on the spot after defeat instead of going back to Headquarters; it costs Energy, so only use it when needed.
R4s & Legion commanders make sure strong players are assigned Captain, so they generate more Energy.

### BOOKING GUIDE — page "How to book Chief Minister / Noble Advisor" (booking-guide.html?lang=en#pm or #adv), a public page opened from the "How to book" buttons in the KvK checklist pop-up. Has annotated screenshots.
Both are 30-minute king's appointments you can book ahead of time. Book the Chief Minister for the moment you spend Truegold / speedups, and the Noble Advisor for your big troop training.
Chief Minister: Construction / Research / Training speed +10%. Noble Advisor: Training speed +50%, Training capacity +200.
Steps: 1. On the city screen tap Events (top right). 2. Pick the Castle Battle tab (the castle icon). 3. Tap Details next to Appointment. 4. In the Ministers tab, tap the position you want. 5. Pick a time slot and tap the button on the right to book it. The list shows your phone's local time; the small button next to the date switches it between local time and UTC. To cancel, open the same window again.
Tips: one booking per position at a time. Each slot shows how many players already applied.

### SQUAD GUIDE — page "How to save a Formation" (squad-guide.html?lang=en), a public page linked from the Formations & Rally Tips guide (guide #formations-rally-tips) right after the recommended presets list; the Bear Hunt guide's joiner rules link to that guide. Annotated screenshots.
Officers often ask for a fixed squad — either an exact troop count (e.g. “exactly 90,000”) or a ratio (e.g. “50 / 25 / 25”). Save it as a Formation once, then just pick that slot when you march or join a rally.
Steps: 1. On the city screen tap your avatar (top left). 2. Tap Squad at the bottom. 3. In Troops Preview, tap Formations. 4. Tap the hero cards and pick the heroes you want in this squad. 5. Tap Save. Done — next time just pick this slot when marching or joining a rally. If a troop COUNT was given: Tap the number on each troop row and type the exact amount. The total shows at the top. If only a RATIO was given: Tap the ratio button at the bottom and enter the percentages — the game fills the troops for you. Tap Save, pick the slot and name it (1–3 letters/numbers). Then choose HOW to save: Count given → Save by Troop Quantity; Ratio given → Save by Troop Ratio (the squad scales with your troops). Tap Confirm. Done — next time just pick this slot when marching or joining a rally.
Tips: the lock icon next to the last slot means it still has to be unlocked. “Even” fills all three types equally; “Withdraw all” clears the slot. Each slot keeps its own heroes and troops.
