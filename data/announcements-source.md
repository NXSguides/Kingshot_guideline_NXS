  **On 30.11.2026, **

  The expansion of the Governor Charm level cap from 11 to 22 is a major progression update in *Kingshot*, significantly increasing the potential power of your troops through the Lethality and Health stats. Reaching the new maximum level of 22 provides a substantial **+99.00% bonus to both Lethality and Health per charm piece**.

  Here is a breakdown of what this cap expansion means, the costs involved, and how to plan your upgrades effectively.


  ### 📈 Key Stats & Unlock Requirement

  The most crucial detail is that while the cap is now 22, the Governor Charm system itself does not become available until you have upgraded your **Town Center to Level 25**. Before this, you can begin stockpiling the necessary materials from various events.

  *   **Maximum Level:** 22
  *   **Unlock Requirement:** Town Center Level 25
  *   **Max Stat Bonus (Level 22):** +99.00% Lethality and +99.00% Health per charm
  *   **Total Charms:** 54 (18 per troop type: Infantry, Archer, Cavalry)


  ### 💰 Upgrade Cost & Stat Progression (Levels 12–22)

  The journey from level 12 to 22 is a significant investment. Each level requires two materials: **Charm Guides** and **Charm Designs**. The costs and stat gains are not linear; you can see the full breakdown for this expanded level range in the table below.

  ### 📊 Total Material Cost (Levels 1–22)

  To fully max out a single charm from level 1 to 22, the total material requirement is substantial:
  *   **Total Charm Guides:** 10,880
  *   **Total Charm Designs:** 19,200

  This highlights that reaching level 22 is a long-term goal, and the costs escalate steeply in the final levels.

  💡 Strategic Takeaways


  Understanding the numbers allows for more efficient planning. Here are some key strategic points to consider:

  *   **Early Levels Offer Better ROI:** The lower levels (especially up to 10 or 11) are significantly cheaper relative to the stats they provide. For players looking for efficient power gains, getting all charms to a baseline level (like 5 or 10) is a high-value strategy before pushing individual charms to the maximum.
  *   **Prioritize Lethality and Health:** Charms are a top-tier investment because they boost Lethality and Health, which are generally more impactful in the damage formula than the Attack and Defense stats provided by Governor Gear.
  *   **Mind the Material Sources:** Charm Guides and Charm Designs are primarily earned through events like Eternity's Reach, Swordland Shop, Alliance Brawl, and Hall of Governors. Plan your participation in these events to sustain your upgrade progress.
  *   **Time Upgrades for Events:** For maximum efficiency, time your charm upgrades to coincide with point-scoring events like "Hall of Governors" or "Kingdom of Power." This way, you can earn valuable secondary rewards for materials you were going to spend anyway.

  In summary, the level cap expansion to 22 offers a clear path to a powerful **+99% Lethality and Health** bonus per charm. However, it is a resource-intensive, long-term endeavor that requires strategic planning, prioritizing high-ROI early levels, and consistent material collection from events.
